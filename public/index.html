About Princeton

One Address. Every Celebration.

Between two metro stations, with direct access from ORR and Vijayawada Highway, Princeton Convention Centre is built for weddings, receptions, conferences, exhibitions and grand celebrations in East Hyderabad.

01

Three Versatile Halls

Choose Your Hall

Three halls, each with its own character. Bookable independently or combined into a single grand floor.

01
200 to 400 Guests

Ruby

Flagship, Grand Weddings and Corporate Galas

High ceilings with crystal chandeliers set the tone before a single decoration goes up. Ruby comes with a built-in dance floor and stage setup, the natural choice for weddings, receptions and corporate galas that need presence without extra effort.

  • High ceilings with crystal chandeliers
  • Built-in dance floor
  • Stage setup included
  • Fully air-conditioned
Book Ruby
02
500 to 800 Guests

Muktha

Elegant, Intimate Weddings and Corporate Meetings

Muktha combines warmth with function. Natural lighting through the day, a premium sound system, projector screen and a dedicated green room. Whether a close-knit reception, engagement ceremony, product launch or leadership offsite, Muktha delivers.

  • Natural lighting throughout the day
  • Premium sound system
  • Projector screen included
  • Dedicated private green room
Book Muktha
03
200 to 1,200 Guests

Kohinoor

Most Versatile, Exhibitions, Trade Shows and Conferences

Kohinoor is Princeton's most adaptable hall. The modular setup accommodates exhibition booths, trade show floor plans and banquet-style wedding seating for 1,200. A registration desk area and dedicated storage make it the practical choice for large events.

  • Fully modular layout
  • Exhibition booth arrangements
  • Registration desk area
  • Dedicated storage space
Book Kohinoor
